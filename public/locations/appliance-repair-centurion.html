Appliance Electrical Faults After Power Interruptions

Power interruptions and voltage fluctuations can sometimes affect household appliances, particularly modern appliances with electronic control boards. Refrigerators, washing machines, dishwashers and other appliances can develop electrical faults after an unstable power event.

If an appliance stops working or develops an electrical fault after a power interruption, our technicians can inspect the appliance and identify the cause of the problem. We can also advise you on suitable surge protection for your appliances to help reduce the risk of future electrical damage.
