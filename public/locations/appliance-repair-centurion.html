Protecting Your Centurion Household from Power Surge Damage

Power interruptions and voltage fluctuations can sometimes affect household appliances, particularly newer models with electronic control boards. Refrigerators, washing machines and other modern appliances rely on sensitive electrical components that can be affected by unstable power.

If an appliance stops working or develops an electrical fault after a power interruption, our technicians can inspect the appliance and identify the cause of the problem. We can also advise you on suitable surge protection for your appliances to help reduce the risk of future electrical damage.
