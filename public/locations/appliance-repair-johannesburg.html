Appliance Repair in Johannesburg

MyFixer provides appliance repair in Johannesburg for homes, offices, property managers and businesses. Fridges, washing machines, dishwashers, aircon systems, stoves and ovens can be repaired on site where practical, while microwaves, TVs and coffee machines are repaired at our workshop.

We repair a wide range of household appliances. The appliance is assessed first so the fault can be identified and the correct repair method—on site or workshop-based—can be explained before repair options are discussed.

Common replacement parts may be available during the visit, while other parts can be sourced when required. Some faults may require additional testing or repair away from the site, depending on the appliance and the nature of the fault. We will explain the next step before proceeding.

How Our Appliance Repair Service Works

Our repair process identifies the appliance fault first and then determines whether on-site or workshop-based repair is the most practical option.

1. Contact MyFixer

Tell us which appliance is giving you trouble and the symptoms you are experiencing.

2. Technician Visit

A technician travels to your home or business for an appliance assessment where On-Site service is available.

3. Fault Diagnosis

The appliance is checked to identify the likely cause of the fault and determine the repair required.

4. Repair Options

Where practical, the repair can be completed during the visit. If further work is required, the next step is explained before proceeding.
