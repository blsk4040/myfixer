Common Fridge & Freezer Problems We Repair

Fridge Not Cooling

If your fridge is running but the food is not staying cold, the problem could involve the thermostat, compressor, start relay, fan, cooling system or another component. We diagnose the cause before recommending the appropriate repair.

Freezer Not Freezing

A freezer that is not freezing properly can have problems with temperature control, airflow, the defrost system, compressor or cooling circuit. We inspect the appliance to identify the fault.

Fridge Water Leaks

Water inside or underneath a refrigerator may be caused by blocked drain channels, drain pans, water lines or defrost-related problems. We locate the source of the leak and diagnose the underlying issue.

Fridge Freezing Up

Excessive frost or ice can indicate a problem with the defrost system, thermostat, temperature sensor, airflow or another refrigeration component.

Compressor Problems

Clicking, humming, repeated starting attempts or a compressor that runs continuously can indicate a refrigeration-system problem. We diagnose compressor and related starting faults.

Electrical & Temperature Problems

We diagnose electrical, thermostat, control-board, wiring, temperature-control and other faults that may prevent the refrigerator from operating correctly.

When Should You Call a Fridge Repair Technician?

You should consider arranging a professional inspection if your refrigerator is no longer cooling properly, the freezer is not freezing, food is becoming warm, water is leaking, excessive ice is forming, the compressor is making unusual noises or the appliance repeatedly switches on and off.

Continuing to operate a refrigerator with a serious cooling or electrical fault can sometimes make the problem worse. A proper diagnosis can help determine whether the appliance can be repaired and what work is required.
