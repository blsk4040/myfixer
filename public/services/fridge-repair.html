Fridge & Freezer Repair

A fridge or freezer that stops working can be a real inconvenience, especially when you have food that needs to stay cold. Whether your fridge is not cooling, making unusual noises, leaking water or building up too much ice, MyFixer can inspect the appliance, identify the fault and advise on the right repair.

We provide mobile fridge and freezer repair services in Centurion, Pretoria, Johannesburg and surrounding areas. Our technicians come to your home or business to check the appliance, explain the problem and, where possible, carry out the repair during the visit.

We work on different types of fridges and freezers, including single-door, double-door, French-door and chest freezer models. We also service popular brands such as Samsung, Defy, Hisense and Bosch, as well as other makes and models.

Fridge & Freezer Problems We Fix

Fridge Not Cooling

We check the thermostat, compressor, start relay and cooling system to find out why the fridge is not staying cold.

Too Much Ice Build-Up

We check the defrost system, temperature sensors and other components when excessive ice builds up inside the fridge or freezer.

Cooling System & Refrigerant Problems

We inspect the cooling system for refrigerant leaks and other faults that may be affecting the fridge's ability to stay cold.

Compressor Problems

We diagnose compressor faults, unusual clicking or humming sounds, starting problems and other issues affecting the cooling system.

Water Leaks

We check blocked drain channels, drain pans, water lines and other components when water is collecting inside or underneath the fridge.

Electrical Problems

We diagnose power, thermostat, wiring, control board and other electrical faults that may prevent the fridge from operating correctly.
