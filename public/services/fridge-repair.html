Fridge & Freezer Repair

A fridge or freezer that stops working can be a real inconvenience, especially when you have food that needs to stay cold. Whether your fridge is not cooling, making unusual noises, leaking water or building up too much ice, MyFixer can inspect the appliance and advise you on the repair.

We provide mobile fridge and freezer repairs for homes and businesses in Centurion, Pretoria, Johannesburg and surrounding areas. Our technicians come to you, check the appliance and explain what needs to be done before carrying out the repair where possible.

We work on different types of fridges and freezers, including single-door, double-door, French-door and chest freezer models. We also repair popular brands such as Samsung, Defy, Hisense and Bosch, as well as other makes and models.

Fridge & Freezer Problems We Fix

Cooling & Temperature Loss

Troubleshooting warm compartments, replacing old thermostats, and fixing faulty start relays.

Excessive Ice Build-Up

Repairing broken automatic defrost heaters, temperature sensors, and faulty timer units.

Gas Leak Detection & Refills

Locating system leaks, sealing copper lines, and recharging eco-safe R600a and R134a refrigerant gas.

Compressor Replacements

Diagnosing continuous motor clicking, unsticking compressor pumps, and installing original motors.

Internal & Base Leaking

Clearing blocked internal drain channels and replacing cracked drain pans or ice-maker connections.

Electrical Power Failures

Pinpointing short circuits causing main board trips and repairing main electronic control cards.
