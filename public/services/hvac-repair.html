Common Aircon Problems We Repair

Aircon Not Cooling

We diagnose restricted airflow, dirty filters, blocked coils, refrigerant-related problems and other cooling system faults.

Poor Airflow

We check indoor fan motors, blower components, filters and airflow pathways when an aircon is not distributing air properly.

Refrigerant Leaks & Cooling Faults

We inspect the system for refrigerant-related faults, assess operating pressures and recharge the system where appropriate.

Electrical & Power Problems

We diagnose power and electrical faults involving capacitors, wiring, controls and other components that can prevent the unit from operating.

Fan & Compressor Problems

We inspect fan motors, bearings, thermal protection and compressor operation when the system develops mechanical or cooling faults.

Water Leaks & Drainage Problems

We diagnose blocked condensate drains, drainage problems and indoor water leaks caused by issues within the aircon system.

Excessive or Unusual Noises

We investigate unusual sounds caused by fan components, loose parts, bearings, mounting issues or compressor-related faults.

Control, PCB & Error Code Problems

We diagnose control faults, PCB problems, unresponsive controls and digital display error codes where applicable.

Why is my aircon not cooling properly?

Inadequate cooling can be caused by restricted airflow, dirty filters or coils, low refrigerant caused by a leak, electrical component faults, fan motor problems or compressor issues. A professional diagnosis helps identify the underlying cause.

Do you provide refrigerant leak diagnosis and recharging?

Yes. Where applicable, we inspect the system and pipework for refrigerant leaks, assess operating pressures and recharge the system using the refrigerant specified for the aircon unit.
