Common Aircon Problems We Repair

Aircon Not Cooling

We diagnose aircon units that are not producing sufficient cooling by checking airflow, filters, coils, refrigerant-related problems and other cooling system faults.

Poor Airflow

We check indoor fan motors, blower components, filters and airflow pathways when an aircon is not distributing air properly.

Refrigerant Problems

We assess refrigerant-related faults, inspect for possible leaks and check system operation before recommending appropriate refrigerant service.

Electrical & Power Problems

We diagnose power and electrical faults involving wiring, capacitors, controls and other components that can prevent the aircon from operating correctly.

Fan & Compressor Problems

We inspect fan motors, bearings, thermal protection and compressor operation when the system develops mechanical or cooling faults.

Aircon Water Leaks

We diagnose blocked condensate drains, drainage problems and indoor water leaks caused by faults within the aircon system.

Excessive or Unusual Noises

We investigate unusual sounds caused by fan components, loose parts, bearings, mounting issues or compressor-related faults.

Control, PCB & Error Code Problems

We diagnose control faults, PCB problems, unresponsive controls and digital display error codes where applicable.
