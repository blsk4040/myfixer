Aircon & Repair Services

An aircon that is not cooling properly can make your home or workplace uncomfortable. Whether your unit is blowing warm air, leaking water, making unusual noises or showing an error, MyFixer can inspect the unit and advise on the repair.

We provide aircon repair and servicing for homes and businesses in Centurion and Pretoria, with selected areas around Johannesburg also covered.

Our technicians work on common aircon problems and different types of units, including wall-mounted split systems, ducted aircon and inverter models. We work with popular brands such as Samsung, LG, Daikin, Midea, Defy, Kelvinator, Fujitsu and Panasonic.

Common Problems We Repair

Aircon Not Cooling

Diagnosing restricted airflow, dirty filters, clogged coils, or underlying cooling cycle failures.

Poor Airflow

Checking indoor fan motors, blower wheels, and air circulation pathways to restore proper room distribution.

Refrigerant Leaks & Cooling Faults

Locating system gas leaks, verifying operating pressures, and recharging refrigerant where necessary.

Electrical & Power Problems

Testing start capacitors, contactors, wiring connections, and resolving units that fail to turn on.

Fan & Compressor Problems

Inspecting condenser fan motors, worn bearings, thermal overloads, and compressor operation issues.

Water Leaks & Drainage Problems

Clearing blocked condensate drain lines, cleaning drain pans, and addressing indoor water dripping.

Excessive Noise / Unusual Noises

Investigating loose fan blades, unaligned mounts, worn motor bearings, or abnormal compressor noises.

Control, PCB & Error Code Problems

Diagnosing printed circuit board (PCB) failures, unresponsive remote controls, and digital display error codes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What aircon problems does MyFixer repair?

We diagnose and repair aircon units that fail to cool, suffer from restricted airflow, experience refrigerant leaks, leak water inside, show electrical or power issues, produce excessive noise, or present control and PCB error codes.

Which aircon brands do you repair?

We service major residential and commercial aircon brands including Samsung, LG, Daikin, Midea, Kelvinator, Defy, Fujitsu, and Panasonic.

Do you provide aircon repair in Centurion and Pretoria?

Yes, Centurion and Pretoria are our primary service areas. We also service selected areas around Johannesburg and surrounding areas in Gauteng.

Can you repair an aircon at my home or business?

Yes, our technicians conduct on-site diagnostic and repair visits for both residential homes and commercial office environments.

Why is my aircon not cooling properly?

Inadequate cooling is often caused by low refrigerant levels due to a leak, dirty air filters or coils, a failing capacitor, fan motor issues, or compressor faults. A professional diagnosis helps identify the root cause.

Do you provide refrigerant leak diagnosis and recharging?

Yes, we inspect pipework and components to identify refrigerant leaks, inspect pressure levels, and recharge system gas using standard refrigerants applicable to your unit.
