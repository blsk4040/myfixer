TV Repair Services

Common TV Problems We Repair

Black Screen & No Picture

Diagnosing TVs that have a black or blank screen, including possible backlight, power supply, display or internal board faults.

Sound & Audio Problems

Checking TVs with no sound, distorted audio, intermittent sound or other common audio-related problems.

TV Not Turning On

Diagnosing power-related faults, standby problems and other issues that prevent the TV from switching on normally.

Picture & Display Problems

Investigating lines, flickering, unusual colours, dim pictures and other display problems to identify the underlying fault.

Smart TV & Connectivity Problems

Helping diagnose common Smart TV issues involving Wi-Fi, apps, software, network connections and other connectivity problems.

HDMI & Connection Faults

Checking faulty or unresponsive HDMI ports, USB connections and other common input and connection problems.
