Coffee Machine Repair Services

A coffee machine that stops working can be a real inconvenience, especially at home or in the office. Whether your machine is not heating, leaking water, making unusual noises or simply not making coffee properly, MyFixer can help diagnose the problem and advise on the right repair.

We provide mobile coffee machine repair services in Centurion, Pretoria and surrounding areas. Our technicians come to your home or business to inspect the machine, identify the fault and, where possible, carry out the repair on site.

We work on a range of domestic and commercial coffee machines, including bean-to-cup machines, capsule machines and home espresso machines. We service popular brands such as DeLonghi, Jura, Nespresso, Saeco, Breville and Gaggia, as well as other makes and models.

Common Coffee Machine Problems We Repair

Water Flow Disruptions

Unclogging internal line blockages and repairing faulty high-pressure water pumps.

Temperature Anomalies

Replacing burnt-out heating elements, boilers, and faulty thermal sensors.

Structural Base Leaking

Replacing worn-out O-rings, degraded silicone seals, and cracked valve connectors.

Grinder Operational Failures

Clearing stone jams, recalibrating burrs, and fixing unreactive grinder motors.

Steam & Frothing Issues

Troubleshooting unreactive steam wands and clearing milk-frother line obstructions.

Digital Display Faults

Diagnosing continuous warning alarms and resetting framework electronic error codes.
