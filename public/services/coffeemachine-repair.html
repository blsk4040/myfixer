Coffee Machine Repair in Centurion, Pretoria & Johannesburg

A coffee machine that suddenly stops working can be frustrating, especially when it is used every day at home, in an office or in a business. Whether your machine is not heating, leaking water, making unusual noises, producing weak coffee or failing to start, MyFixer provides professional coffee machine repair and servicing to help identify the problem and recommend the appropriate repair.

We operate as a mobile appliance repair service, providing coffee machine diagnostics and on-site repairs where practical. Our technicians can travel to homes, offices and commercial premises in Centurion, Pretoria, Johannesburg and surrounding areas.

Depending on the fault and machine type, the technician will inspect the appliance, identify the likely cause of the problem and explain the recommended repair before additional work is carried out. Some specialised repairs may require the machine to be taken to a suitable workshop.

Common Coffee Machine Problems We Repair

Machine Not Heating

We diagnose heating problems involving boilers, heating elements, thermostats, temperature sensors and related components.

Water Flow & Pump Problems

We check pumps, water lines, valves and other components when water flow is weak, restricted or not working.

Water Leaks

We investigate leaks caused by worn seals, O-rings, hoses, valves, connectors and other internal components.

Grinder Problems

We diagnose blocked grinders, worn burrs, inconsistent grinding and grinder motor problems where repair is practical.

Steam & Milk Frother Problems

We inspect steam wands and milk frothers affected by blockages, poor steam pressure, valves and other faults.

Electrical & Control Faults

We diagnose power problems, control faults, warning messages and machines that fail to start or operate correctly.
