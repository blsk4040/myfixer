Coffee Machine Repair

A coffee machine that stops working can be a real inconvenience, especially at home or in the office. Whether your machine is not heating, leaking water, making unusual noises or simply not making coffee properly, MyFixer can help diagnose the problem and recommend the right repair.

We provide mobile coffee machine repair services in Centurion, Pretoria and surrounding areas. Our technicians come to your home or business to inspect the machine, identify the fault and, where possible, carry out the repair on site.

We work on a range of domestic and commercial coffee machines, including bean-to-cup machines, capsule machines and home espresso machines. We service popular brands such as DeLonghi, Jura, Nespresso, Saeco, Breville and Gaggia, as well as other makes and models.

Common Coffee Machine Problems We Repair

Water Flow Problems

We repair coffee machines with poor water flow, blocked lines, weak pressure, faulty pumps or water delivery problems.

Heating Problems

We diagnose and repair machines that are not heating properly, including faulty heating elements, boilers, thermostats and temperature sensors.

Water Leaks

We repair leaks caused by worn seals, O-rings, hoses, valves, connectors and other internal components.

Grinder Problems

We diagnose grinder faults, including blocked grinders, worn burrs, inconsistent grinding and grinder motor problems.

Steam & Frothing Problems

We repair steam wands and milk frothers affected by blockages, poor steam pressure, faulty valves and other common faults.

Electrical & Control Faults

We diagnose power problems, control panel faults, warning messages, electronic issues and machines that fail to start or operate correctly.
