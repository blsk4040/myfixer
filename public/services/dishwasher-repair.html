Common Dishwasher Problems We Repair

Dishwasher Not Draining

We check the drain pump, filter, hoses and other parts of the drainage system to find the cause of standing water.

Water Leaks

We find the source of leaks around the door seal, hoses, valves, gaskets and other internal components.

Poor Cleaning

We check the spray arms, filters, water circulation and other components when dishes are left dirty or covered in residue.

Dishwasher Not Starting

We check the power supply, door latch, controls and other components when the dishwasher won't start.

Heating Problems

We diagnose heating faults involving the heating element, thermostat, temperature sensor and related components.

Error Codes & Faults

We diagnose error codes and other electrical or mechanical faults that prevent the dishwasher from working correctly.
