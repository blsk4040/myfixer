Common Dishwasher Problems We Repair

Dishwasher Not Draining

Our technicians inspect the drainage system, pump, hoses, and related components to identify and resolve the cause of standing water.

Water Leaks

We trace the source of water leakage around door seals, internal hoses, valves, and gaskets to restore watertight operation.

Poor Cleaning Results

We evaluate wash arms, filtration systems, and internal circulation components to address spotting, residue, and weak spray performance.

Dishwasher Not Starting

We check electrical connections, door latch mechanisms, and control systems to diagnose why a cycle won't initiate.

Heating Problems

We inspect heating elements and temperature regulation components so your dishwasher reaches the right water temperature for cleaning.

Error Codes & Faults

We read system fault codes and test internal sensors to pinpoint hidden electrical or mechanical issues causing operation errors.
