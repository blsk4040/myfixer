Common Dishwasher Problems We Repair

Dishwasher Not Draining

We diagnose dishwashers that leave standing water inside the machine by checking the drain pump, filter, drain hose and other parts of the drainage system.

Dishwasher Leaking

We investigate water leaks around the door seal, hoses, valves, gaskets and other internal components to identify where the leak is coming from.

Dishwasher Not Cleaning Properly

We check spray arms, filters, water circulation, water flow and related components when dishes remain dirty, greasy or covered in residue after a cycle.

Dishwasher Not Starting

We diagnose problems involving the power supply, door latch, controls, switches and other components when the dishwasher will not start or complete a cycle.

Dishwasher Not Heating

We diagnose heating faults involving the heating element, thermostat, temperature sensor and related components when water does not reach the required temperature.

Error Codes & Faults

We diagnose dishwasher error codes and electrical or mechanical faults that prevent the appliance from operating correctly.

Dishwasher Diagnostics & Servicing
