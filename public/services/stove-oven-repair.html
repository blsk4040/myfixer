Common Stove & Oven Problems We Repair

Stove Plates Not Heating

Diagnosing and replacing faulty solid plates, spiral elements, or defective internal switches on electric hobs.

Oven Not Heating Properly

Testing and repairing failed bake or grill elements, faulty thermostats, and defective temperature sensors.

Gas Burner & Ignition Problems

Resolving gas ignition faults, blocked burner jets, and faulty safety flame detectors.

Oven Door, Hinge & Seal Problems

Replacing worn door rubber seals and loose hinges to stop heat escaping during cooking.

Oven Fan & Convection Problems

Repairing noisy, stuck, or non-functional fan motors to ensure proper heat circulation.

Control Panel & Timer Faults

Addressing unresponsive digital displays, timer errors, and faulty control knobs or relays.

An oven that does not heat properly may have a faulty heating element, thermostat, temperature sensor, wiring or control component. Professional diagnosis can identify the underlying fault.
