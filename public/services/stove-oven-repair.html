Common Stove & Oven Problems We Repair

Stovetop Plates Not Heating

Replacing burnt electric spiral rings, broken solid plates, and cracked glass under-surface ceramic elements.

Uneven Oven Thermal Profiles

Diagnosing faulty top bake or lower grill elements, testing safety thermostats, and balancing heat levels.

Gas Ignition Failures

Cleaning clogged burner injectors, replacing worn electronic click igniters, and installing safety thermocouples.

Oven Door Hinge & Seal Gaps

Fitting high-temperature perimeter rubber seals and replacing worn door hinges to lock thermal heat back inside.

Convection Fan Vibrations

Replacing short-circuiting rear fan motors, oiling dry axle points, and tightening loose impeller fan blades.

Digital Controller Board Errors

Clearing random dashboard error locks, matching display relays, and replacing faulty temperature probe sensors.
