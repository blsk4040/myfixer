Common Microwave Problems We Repair

No Heating / Power Failure

Replacing burnt-out high-voltage magnetrons, shorted diodes, and failing primary transformers safely.

Internal Sparking & Arcing

Installing replacement waveguide cover plates, treating exposed cavity lining paint, and replacing metal rollers.

Turntable Rotation Faults

Testing under-tray synchronous drive motors, fitting new guide couplings, and changing cracked glass trays.

Door Switch & Latch Faults

Re-aligning door hooks and replacing faulty micro-interlock switches to restore system connection safety.

Loud Humming & Buzzing

Replacing weak high-voltage capacitors and balancing faulty cooling fan blades inside the structural housing.

Control Panel Display Blanks

Diagnosing non-responsive membrane keypads, clearing integrated circuit panel faults, and clearing software locks.
