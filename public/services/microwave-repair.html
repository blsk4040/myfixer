Microwave Repair Services

A microwave that stops heating, sparks, makes unusual noises or has problems with the door can be frustrating. MyFixer provides mobile microwave repair services for homes and businesses, with technicians who can inspect the appliance and identify the fault.

We provide microwave repairs in Centurion, Pretoria and Johannesburg, as well as selected surrounding areas in Gauteng. On-site diagnostics and repairs are provided where practical.

We repair countertop and built-in microwaves from popular brands including Samsung, LG, Defy, Russell Hobbs, Midea, Bosch, Panasonic, Sharp and Hisense . Common faults include microwaves that are not heating, will not switch on, have faulty controls, or have problems with the door or turntable.

Microwaves contain high-voltage components, so internal repairs should be carried out by a qualified technician. Our technicians follow appropriate safety procedures when inspecting and repairing these appliances.

Common Microwave Problems We Repair

Microwave Not Heating

Diagnosing heating-system faults and electrical components that can prevent the microwave from producing heat.

Sparking Inside the Microwave

Inspecting common causes of sparking, including damaged internal components, waveguide covers and damaged internal surfaces.

Turntable Not Working

Diagnosing turntable motors, couplings, rollers and related mechanical faults that prevent the tray from rotating correctly.

Door, Latch & Safety Switch Problems

Checking door alignment, latches and safety interlock switches when the microwave does not operate correctly.

Unusual Humming or Buzzing

Investigating unusual operating noises and checking relevant electrical, fan and internal components.

Control Panel & Display Faults

Diagnosing unresponsive buttons, display problems and control-system faults affecting normal operation.
