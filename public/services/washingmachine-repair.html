Common Washing Machine Problems We Repair

Drum Failures & No Spin

We inspect the drive system, motor, bearings and related components to identify the cause of spin problems and unusual drum behaviour.

Water Leaks & Seal Problems

Inspecting door seals, internal hoses, inlet valves and pumps to locate and repair common sources of water leakage.

Drainage Blocks & Pump Faults

Checking drain filters, hoses and pumps to identify blockages or component faults that prevent the machine from draining properly.

Electrical & Control Faults

Diagnosing power problems, electrical faults, door interlocks, heating components and electronic control issues.

Loud Noise & Vibration

Checking suspension components, shock absorbers, bearings, counterweights and levelling issues that may cause excessive noise or movement.

Error Codes & Faults

Diagnosing error codes and testing sensors, switches, valves and other components to identify the underlying operating fault.
