Common Washing Machine Problems We Repair

Drum Failures & No Spin

Replacing snapped drive belts, installing new carbon motor brushes, and fixing worn-down main drum bearings.

Water Leaks & Seal Fractures

Replacing torn door rubber bellow boots, fixing split internal feed hoses, and replacing failing water inlet valves.

Drainage Blocks & Pump Failures

Clearing blocked coin traps, renewing burnt electric drain pumps, and removing obstacles from internal waste lines.

Electrical Trips & Board Faults

Isolating short-circuited water heating elements, updating corrupted control PCBs, and replacing broken door locks.

Loud Operational Vibration

Installing replacement hydraulic suspension shock absorbers, fitting new stabilization springs, and rebalancing internal concrete counterweights.

System Error Codes

Interpreting digital control panel error triggers, replacing faulty pressure switches, and correcting thermal monitoring sensors.
