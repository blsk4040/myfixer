Common Washing Machine Problems We Repair

Drum Failures & No Spin

We inspect the drive system, motor, bearings and related components to identify the cause of spin problems and unusual drum behaviour.

Water Leaks & Seal Fractures

Inspecting door seals, internal hoses, inlet valves, and pumps to locate and repair water leaks safely.

Drainage Blocks & Pump Failures

Clearing blockages, checking drain filters, and replacing malfunctioning drain pumps to restore proper water evacuation.

Electrical Trips & Board Faults

Diagnosing power issues, electrical shorts, door interlocks, heating elements, and electronic control modules.

Loud Operational Vibration

Checking shock absorbers, suspension springs, counterweights, and leveling feet to reduce excessive noise and shaking.

System Error Codes

Reading electronic fault codes and testing sensors, switches, and valves to resolve operating errors.
