Common Fridge & Refrigerator Problems

A fridge that is not cooling properly, leaking or making unusual noises can have several possible causes. These checks can help you understand the symptom before arranging professional diagnosis.

Common Fridge Problems

Fridge Not Cooling Properly

Check that the temperature setting is correct and that food is not blocking internal air vents. Dirty condenser areas, airflow problems, fan faults, defrost problems, refrigerant issues and compressor faults can also affect cooling and require proper diagnosis.

Excessive Frost or Ice Buildup

Heavy frost can restrict airflow and may be linked to a defrost system fault, door-seal problem, temperature-control issue or another underlying fault. A manual defrost may provide temporary relief, but recurring frost should be assessed.

Water Leaking

Water inside or underneath a fridge can be related to a blocked drain, drain-pan issue, door seal, water connection or another component. Check for obvious food or ice blocking the drain area, but persistent leaks should be professionally assessed.

Unusual Buzzing or Grinding

Some compressor and fan sounds are normal, but a new or unusually loud buzzing, rattling or grinding noise can indicate a fan, compressor or other mechanical or electrical problem.

Fridge Running Constantly

A fridge may run for long periods when the room is hot or when it has recently been loaded with warm food. If it runs continuously and still struggles to maintain temperature, possible causes include poor door sealing, airflow problems, sensor or control faults, refrigerant problems or compressor issues.

Maintenance Tip: Keep condenser areas, vents and the space around the appliance clean and unobstructed according to the manufacturer's maintenance instructions.

Common Washing Machine Problems

Washing machines can stop spinning, fail to drain, leak, shake excessively or refuse to start. The correct diagnosis depends on the symptoms and machine design.

Common Washing Machine Problems

Washing Machine Not Spinning

An overloaded or unbalanced load can prevent proper spinning. If the machine still will not spin with a balanced load, the cause may involve the door or lid lock, motor, belt, drain system, control system or another component.

Water Leaking

Check accessible inlet and drain hoses for visible cracks, loose connections or damage. Water can also leak from internal hoses, pumps, seals or other components and may require professional diagnosis.

Machine Shaking or Moving

Excessive movement can result from an unbalanced load, an uneven floor or incorrect machine levelling. Persistent severe vibration may indicate worn suspension, shock absorbers, bearings or another mechanical fault.

Washing Machine Won't Start

Check that the appliance is receiving power and that the door or lid is properly closed. If the machine powers on but does not begin the cycle, the door lock, control system, water supply or another component may need to be checked.

Bad Smell From the Drum

Detergent residue, moisture and buildup can cause unpleasant smells. Use the machine's cleaning cycle or a manufacturer-approved washing machine cleaner according to the appliance instructions, and leave the door open after use where appropriate.

Maintenance Tip: Avoid overloading the machine and follow the manufacturer's recommended load size, detergent quantity and maintenance instructions.

Common TV Problems

A TV can have picture, sound, power or source-related problems. Some symptoms point to external connections, while others require professional electronic diagnosis.

Common TV Problems

Black Screen but Sound Works

If the TV produces sound but no visible picture, possible causes include the backlight, power supply, display circuitry or another internal component. Check the input source and connections first. Persistent black-screen faults require proper diagnosis.

Lines or Distorted Picture

Lines, blocks or distorted images can be caused by signal connections, processing circuitry, display connections or panel damage. Avoid pressing or hitting the screen, as physical pressure can make panel damage worse.

Screen Flickering

Flickering can be related to the input source, HDMI connection, power supply, backlight, processing circuitry or display panel. Try another input or cable before arranging professional diagnosis.

No Sound

Check the TV volume, mute setting, audio output selection and connected devices. If the settings and source are correct but there is still no sound, an internal audio circuit, speaker or board fault may require repair.

TV Has No Power

Check the wall socket, power connection and remote batteries. If the TV remains completely unresponsive, the power supply, main board or another internal component may require professional assessment.

Maintenance Tip: Keep ventilation openings clear and avoid placing the TV where heat cannot escape.

Common Aircon & HVAC Problems

Air conditioners can develop cooling, airflow, drainage, electrical and control problems. Some basic checks are safe for homeowners, while refrigerant and electrical faults require professional service.

Common Aircon & HVAC Problems

Aircon Blowing Warm Air

Check the temperature setting, operating mode and accessible air filter. Poor airflow, refrigerant problems, compressor faults, electrical issues and control faults can also cause poor cooling and require professional diagnosis.

Ice Buildup on the Unit

Ice can develop when airflow is restricted or when the system has a refrigerant or other operating problem. Switch the system off and allow it to defrost. If icing returns, arrange professional assessment rather than continuing to operate it.

Water Leaking From the Aircon

Indoor water leaks can be related to drainage, installation, condensation or other faults. If the drain system is not easily accessible, avoid dismantling the unit and arrange professional service.

Weak Airflow

A dirty accessible filter, blocked airflow path, fan problem or another internal fault can reduce airflow. Check and clean accessible filters according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Unusual Noises

Rattling, grinding, buzzing or other new noises can indicate loose components, fan problems, compressor issues or electrical faults. Unusual noises that persist should be professionally assessed.

Maintenance Tip: Clean or replace accessible filters according to the manufacturer's maintenance schedule and arrange servicing when cooling or airflow problems persist.

Common Dishwasher Problems

A dishwasher that leaves dishes dirty, fails to drain, leaks or does not dry properly can have several possible causes. Basic checks can help identify the symptom before professional repair.

Common Dishwasher Problems

Dishes Stay Dirty

Check that the spray arms can rotate freely and that filters are clean according to the appliance instructions. Incorrect loading, blocked spray paths, water supply problems or wash-system faults can also affect cleaning performance.

Dishwasher Not Draining

Check the accessible filter and look for obvious blockages. The drain hose, pump, valve or control system may also be involved if water remains in the machine after a cycle.

Water Leaking

A damaged door seal, hose connection, water inlet component or internal part can cause leaks. Stop using the dishwasher if the leak is significant and arrange an assessment.

Dishes Are Not Drying

Poor drying can be related to the selected cycle, rinse-aid settings, loading or the heating and drying system. Follow the manufacturer's drying recommendations before arranging a repair.

Grinding or Unusual Sounds

Foreign objects, spray-arm contact, the circulation system or drain pump can cause unusual sounds. If the noise continues after checking accessible areas, professional inspection may be required.

Maintenance Tip: Clean the dishwasher filter regularly and use detergent and rinse aid according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Common Stove & Oven Problems

Electric and gas cooking appliances can develop heating, ignition, burner, thermostat, electrical, door and control problems. Gas-related faults should be assessed by a suitably qualified technician.

Common Stove & Oven Problems

Oven Not Heating

If the oven powers on but does not heat, possible causes include a heating element, thermostat, temperature sensor, selector, control board or electrical supply problem. Professional diagnosis may be required.

Uneven Cooking

Uneven cooking can be related to temperature settings, incorrect rack position, airflow, heating elements, sensors or the oven control system. An oven thermometer can help identify significant temperature variation.

Gas Burner or Ignition Problem

If a gas burner does not ignite correctly or the flame behaves unusually, stop using the appliance if there is a gas smell or unsafe condition. Do not attempt to repair gas lines, valves or ignition components yourself. Arrange assessment by a suitably qualified technician.

Oven Door Not Closing Properly

A damaged hinge, seal, latch or door alignment problem can prevent proper closure. A poor seal can also affect cooking performance and heat retention.

Burning Smell or Smoke

Small amounts of smoke can occur when food residue burns, but persistent smoke, electrical smells or unusual burning odours should not be ignored. Stop using the appliance and arrange professional inspection when the cause is unclear.

Maintenance Tip: Keep the oven interior clean according to the manufacturer's instructions and avoid blocking vents or airflow openings.

Common Microwave Problems

Microwave faults can involve heating, turntable, controls, door switches or other electrical components. Microwave internals contain hazardous high voltage and should not be opened by unqualified users.

Common Microwave Problems

Microwave Turns On but Does Not Heat

If the microwave runs but food remains cold, the fault may involve the high-voltage system, control system or another internal component. Do not open the appliance yourself. Arrange professional diagnosis.

Sparking Inside the Microwave

Sparking can be caused by unsuitable metal objects, damaged internal surfaces, food residue or component faults. Stop using the microwave if sparking occurs, particularly if it continues after removing an obvious unsuitable object.

Turntable Not Spinning

Check that the turntable is correctly positioned and that the support mechanism is seated properly. If it still does not rotate, the motor, coupling or control system may need assessment.

Loud Buzzing or Unusual Electrical Noise

A sudden loud buzz, crackle or abnormal electrical noise should not be ignored. Stop using the appliance and arrange professional assessment rather than attempting to access internal components.

Door or Latch Problems

If the microwave door does not close or latch correctly, stop using the appliance. Door and interlock systems are important safety components and should be professionally assessed.

Safety Note: Never open a microwave's internal casing yourself. Dangerous high voltage can remain present even after the appliance has been unplugged.

Common Coffee Machine Problems

Coffee machines can develop brewing, heating, grinding, water-flow, leakage and pump-related problems. Correct maintenance depends on the machine type and manufacturer.

Common Coffee Machine Problems

Coffee Machine Not Brewing

Check the water tank, brewing settings and any accessible user-serviceable components. Restricted water flow, scale buildup, pump problems, valves, heating components or control faults can prevent proper brewing.

Coffee Machine Leaking

Water leaks can come from the tank, seals, hoses, connectors, valves or internal components. Check that the water tank is correctly seated and arrange professional assessment if the leak continues.

Grinder Not Working

A grinder may stop because of beans, foreign material, buildup or an internal mechanical or electrical fault. Follow the manufacturer's cleaning instructions and avoid forcing the grinder if it is jammed.

Coffee Is Weak or Not Hot Enough

Weak coffee can result from grind settings, dose, coffee quantity, water temperature, flow rate or machine condition. If the machine is also struggling to heat or brew correctly, professional assessment may be required.

Unusual Pump or Machine Noise

Changes in pump noise can be associated with water flow restrictions, air in the system, scale buildup or a mechanical problem. Follow the manufacturer's priming and cleaning instructions before arranging service.

Maintenance Tip: Use suitable filtered water where recommended and descale and clean the machine according to the manufacturer's instructions and recommended products.
