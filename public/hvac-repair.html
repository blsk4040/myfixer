Common Aircon & HVAC Problems We Repair

  • Not cooling / poor airflow and ventilation
  • Refrigerant leaks / Aircon gas refills (R410a, R22, R32)
  • Electrical faults / outdoor condenser unit won’t start
  • Loud humming or clicking / faulty compressor or fan motor
  • Thermostat malfunctions or connection control errors
  • Blocked drainage lines / water leaking indoors
  • Dirty filters / air quality and indoor coil freezing
  • Ductwork or air balancing vent repairs
